StoryPlay Privacy Policy

Your Choice. Your Story.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

StoryPlay ("the App") is an interactive branching-story streaming app operated by the StoryPlay team. Contact: owner@storyplay.app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: When you sign in, we collect your email address (for email sign-in), phone number (for phone sign-in), or basic profile information from Google (for Google sign-in), as you choose.
  • Usage information: Stories you watch, choices you make, likes, saved stories, watch history, and coin balance, so the app can resume your progress and show your rewards.
  • Device information: Basic device and app information needed to operate the app and deliver updates.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To save your progress, coins, rewards and preferences across devices.
  • To show you stories, trailers and recommendations.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.

3. Data storage

Your data is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) and is not sold to anyone. We do not share your personal information with advertisers.

4. Mature content

Some stories contain mature themes. Mature content is off by default for new accounts where applicable and can be controlled in Settings → Mature Content. The app is rated 18+.

5. Your rights

  • You can sign out at any time from Profile → Settings.
  • You can delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, history, coins and saved stories.
  • You can ask us to delete your data by emailing owner@storyplay.app.

6. Children

StoryPlay is intended for users aged 18 and over.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, where needed, tell you inside the app.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or data request, email owner@storyplay.app.
